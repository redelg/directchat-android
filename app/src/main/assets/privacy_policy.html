Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 27, 2026

Direct Chat is a utility that opens chat links from a phone number entered by the user.

Data We Collect

We do not collect personal identifiers, chat content, or contact lists through this app.

How the App Works

When you enter a number, the app generates a chat link and opens it in a compatible messaging app installed on your device.

Permissions

The app only requests internet access to load ads and open links.

Advertising

The app uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). AdMob may process device and usage data according to Google policies.

Data Storage

The app may keep recent numbers locally on your device to improve usability. This data is not sold and is not shared by the app developer.

Children

This app is not directed to children under 13.

Contact

If you have privacy questions, contact the developer through the support email listed in the store listing.
