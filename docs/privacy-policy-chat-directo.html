Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 27, 2026

App: Chat Directo (com.codergang.chatdirecto)

Developer: North Coast Solutions

Chat Directo is an independent utility app that helps users open direct chat conversations from a phone number without saving it as a contact. This app is not affiliated with or endorsed by any third-party messaging platform.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or store any of the following on our servers:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, phone number)
  • Chat content or message history
  • Contact lists or address book data
  • Location data

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The app stores the following data only on your device using a local database:

  • Recent numbers: Phone numbers you have used are saved locally for quick access. You can delete them at any time from the History tab.
  • Message templates: Custom message templates you create are stored locally.
  • Preferences: App settings such as onboarding status and purchase state.

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to the developer.

3. Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and process certain data according to Google's Privacy Policy, including:

  • Device identifiers (Advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • General usage and interaction data

Users who purchase the ad-free option will no longer see ads, and ad-related data collection by AdMob will cease.

4. In-App Purchases

The app uses RevenueCat to manage in-app purchases through the Google Play billing system. RevenueCat may process:

  • Anonymous app user IDs
  • Purchase and subscription status
  • Transaction receipts for validation

No payment information (credit card numbers, billing addresses) is collected by us. All payment processing is handled by Google Play. For more details, see RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

5. Permissions

The app requests only the INTERNET permission, which is used to:

  • Load advertisements
  • Validate in-app purchases
  • Open chat links in external apps

6. Third-Party Services

The app integrates the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob – Advertising
  • RevenueCat – Purchase management
  • Google Play Services – Billing and app distribution

7. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

All locally stored data (recent numbers, message templates, preferences) can be deleted by:

  • Clearing app data from your device settings
  • Uninstalling the app
  • Deleting individual items within the app

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
